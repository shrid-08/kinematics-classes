Why Kinematics Classes

We Don't Make You Memorize. We Make You Visualize Physics & Science.

Named after Kinematics — the branch of physics that describes the motion of points, bodies, and systems — our philosophy is to keep students perpetually in forward motion toward academic excellence.

Founded in 2014 by a team of enthusiastic IIT graduates, Kinematics Classes has transformed from a humble classroom of 15 dreamers into a premier education landmark that consistently produces state toppers, single-digit AIRs in JEE Advanced, and 680+ scorers in NEET.

Visual & Derivation-Driven Learning: Interactive simulations, real-life examples, and crystal-clear mathematical proofs.
Strict Small Batch Limit (Max 25-30): No 200-student crowded auditoriums where backbenchers are forgotten.
Parent-Mentor Transparency: Bi-weekly performance tracking reports & direct monthly parent-teacher conferences.
